Case #1: The clinic

Jacob Thorne is a drug dealer and is selling to Daniel Harker, who is an addict.

Daniel is owing Jacob money.

The local clinic is currently run by Daniel Harker and Drake Gallows.

They need "Meld" to treat their patients.

Meld is hard to acquire without support from the big corporations.

Daniel took a shortcut and found some local gangsters that could provide the stuff.

Drake has received an offer from Eschaton to rat out Daniel for a big reward.

Daniel found the offer and locked Drake away.
