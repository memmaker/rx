By default, all weapons are vendor locked and will only accept ammo from the same manufacturer.
There is perk to unlock this restriction.

Ammo Types
 - high_velocity -> more noise, easier to hit, more damage & recoil
 - gyrojet -> no recoil, less noise, caseless, expensive, require special weapon, speeds up per distance, so less damage up close
 - polymer casing
 - brass casing
 - caseless -> less jamming
 - flechette -> more range, armor piercing
 - armor_piercing -> less damage, more penetration
 - hollow_point -> more damage, less penetration
 - overpressure (dangerous in low quality weapons)
 - magnum -> more power, more recoil
 - subsonic -> harder to hit, but less noise
 - handloaded -> depend on quality center at 50%

One of two things will happen if you use a small caliber weapon:
80% -> The bullet will ricochet off the lock
20% -> The bullet will lodge itself into the lock and make it even more difficult to open.

Larger Calibers (> .45) will:
60% -> Ricochet off the lock
20% -> Destroy the lock and make it impossible to open.
20% -> Destroy the lock and make it possible to open.

Better:
100% -> Shotgun + Breaching Rounds
80% Shotgun Slug
60% Shotgun Buckshot

    - Activate a shield
