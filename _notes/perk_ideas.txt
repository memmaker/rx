Perks = minimal, localized mechanic changes
Perks = The missing glue to make some iconic characters possible

Quick Draw
 - While you have only one weapon in your inventory, you can issue a quick draw attack that deals 50% more damage.
 - Starts a combo

Style with Substance
 - While wearing fashion clothing without any DT, you gain +4 DT.
 - Any quick draw attack with fashion clothing equipped will deal 100% more damage.

Dodge Bullets
 - You can activate this perk to dodge bullets while running quickly next to an enemy.
 - Keeps combo running

Body Load
 - Reload smart guns from a cybernetically implanted ammo pocket
 - Makes reloads a free action for smart guns at the cost of one action point

Exploder
 - All attacks on explosives trigger them, instead of only incendiary bullets and lasers

Gun Kata
 - You can activate this perk to shoot at multiple enemies in a single turn, while also gaining 50% dodge chance.
 - Requires Pistol, Rifle, SMG, or Shotgun equipped.
 - Requires regular bullets, doesn't work for energy weapons.
 - Keeps combo running if no heavy hit is sustained.

Panzerkunst
 - Augmented melee combat style

Reflector
 - You have a 50% chance to reflect laser beams back to the shooter while wearing metal armor.

Shield of cool
 - Apply coolness as dodge chance.
