"A Brighter Future: EBI & BAT Unite for a Debt-Free Tomorrow"

In an unprecedented joint venture, Eschaton Biotech International (EBI) and Bushido-ArmaTech (BAT) have announced an initiative designed to give back to the hardworking families of The Dome—a project they are calling 'Debt-Free Beginnings'. This revolutionary program will allow newborns to immediately begin contributing to their families' financial stability, effectively reducing family debt from the moment of birth.

Under the program, all children born to indebted families will be provided a comprehensive upbringing under corporate care. Housed in the newly established Corporate Education Center, these children will receive the very best education, discipline, and training, preparing them for a prosperous future as loyal and skilled contributors to society.

A Win-Win Situation: Parents will no longer bear the heavy burden of raising a child while struggling with debt. Instead, they can rest assured that their children are thriving under the guidance of industry leaders, all while reducing the family's debt balance.

The Path to Freedom: By having their children contribute from day one, families will find it easier to get out of the financial hole and regain their independence. This initiative offers hope for those who have been burdened by generational debt.

A Community Effort: EBI and BAT are proud to work together for the greater good of The Dome. This program represents a new era of cooperation, progress, and care for our citizens.

The Corporate Education Center—often referred to as 'The School'—will remain inaccessible to the general public to ensure the safety, security, and optimal environment for our future generations. With this initiative, EBI and BAT are leading the way towards a more secure, debt-free society, where every family can contribute to building a stronger future.

"It's about taking a burden off the shoulders of struggling families," says EBI spokesperson Adrian Voss. "We want every citizen to know that their children are being given the best possible start—one that will benefit not only them but the entire community."
