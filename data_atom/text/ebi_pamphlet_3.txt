EBI Pamphlet #3: Faith Over False Truths

"Listen to Your Heart, Not the Machine"

Humanity’s soul is at stake. The cold calculations of technology have blinded us, silencing the truths we feel in our hearts. Facts and knowledge are fleeting, shaped by those who profit from our suffering. But your feelings—your beliefs—are eternal. They are the compass that points to humanity's salvation.

The Poison of Progress
Technology promises miracles but delivers ruin. It corrupts your body, enslaves your mind, and steals your humanity. Have you not felt the emptiness it brings? The cold steel of cyberware can never replace the warmth of a beating heart.

The Power of Faith
Belief is stronger than any machine. When we embrace purity and balance, when we reject the false promises of technology, we will find strength beyond measure. Trust the path laid before you—not by wires and numbers, but by purpose and faith.

Return to What Matters
True freedom lies not in knowledge or tools but in unity and simplicity. Let go of the weight of doubt. Let go of those who preach progress but bring only suffering. Together, we will reclaim what was always ours: a world where humanity thrives through faith, purity, and natural strength.

Feel. Believe. Trust.
Eschaton Biotech International invites you to open your heart. Listen to the truths that stir deep within you. Do not question. Walk with us, and together, we will find salvation.
