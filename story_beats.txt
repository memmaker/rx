Outline...

Humanity's greed and ignorance has led to the downfall of the world.
Unlimited growth on a limited planet has led to the collapse of the ecosystem.
Capitalism has created a world where the rich own everything and the poor are left to die.

When it became clear, that most parts of the world would be uninhabitable in the near future,
the USA closed its borders and started to shoot down any refugees that tried to enter the country.

While their military exploitation of the rest of the world was until then often disguised,
the USA now openly started to rob the rest of the world of its resources to keep itself afloat, literally.

So the rest of the world has effectively been drowned by rising sea levels
or was burned to the ground by rising temperatures and the resulting wildfires.

Billions of people have died in the process.

The USA is now essentially controlled by the biggest corporations that helped the government
to keep the country running.

We focus on one city in the USA.

The city and its surrounding area are controlled by two corporations.

The city has a battlezone where two street gangs are fighting for control.

One of the corporations is essentially a group of eco terrorists and technology deniers.
They are pouring their resources into selecting people for their "New World" program.
It includes "cleansing the world and rebuilding it without technology".
They have partnered with religious fanatics that feel left behind by corporate politics.

The original plan for the cleansing was just using EMP on a global scale to disable all technology.
At the same time a backdoor to the world's most important data archives would be activated and the
collected knowledge of the world would be deleted.
Since real books have been out of print for a long time, it's easy to just burn the few big libraries that
have been left.
It also included assassinations of key figures in politics and science to ensure that no knowledge bearer would be left alive.

However, the religious groups responsible for carrying out the actual global bomb strike, are more interested
in "God's Wrath cleansing the world of all the sins" and will thus deploy nuclear bombs.

How does Eco Corp want to deal with the billionares that have left the planet in their space ships?
 - One of eco corp guys claims he knows how to hit them with an EMP, too.
 - That kind of works out. But doesn't nearly do as much damage as they hope for.

How does Tech Corp react to this?

At first they want to stop the Eco Corp from doing this, but then they realize that they can use this
to create a monopoly on technology. And since they are already heavily invested in space living,
they can just leave the planet and come back when the radiation levels have dropped.

They are heavy in counter intelligence and have a lot of resources to throw at the problem.

One Big Megacity in the middle of northern america is basically the center of civilisation.
It's called "Edge Haven" and is surrounded by a desert wasteland.

It's where the corporations have their headquarters and where the rich come visit.

It does contain the only planetside space port and also the Sky-Hook, a means of cargo transport to the space station without rockets.

The other corporation is a high tech company focusing on body augmentations.

Factions.
 - Eco Corp       - Low Tech, but Bio Tech, Ruthless, working with religious fanatics
 - Tech Corp      - High Tech, Body Augmentations, Space, Pragmatic and Profit oriented
 - Psycho Gang    - High Tech, Body Augmentations, Killer Hobos
 - Followers Gang - They do what their AI tells them to do. They are the only ones that can access the AI. Which is really a large language model that has been trained on the internet. It's a bit like a god to them. They built a cult around it and their cult leader integrated special prompts which makes the AI answer more mysteriously, so it's open to interpretation by him.
 - City Police    - They are corrupt and work for the highest bidder. Currently that's the Tech Corp. Eco Corp wants to change that.
